Acceptable Use Policy

Effective DECISION: publication date

rewind.js runs your code on shared machines and, on paid plans, sends HTTP requests and email on your behalf. This policy is what you may not do with it. It is part of our Terms of Service and applies to everyone using an account, and to every app deployed on rewind.js.

1. Content and conduct

Do not use rewind.js to host, send or facilitate:

  • anything illegal where you or your users are, or in the United States or Canada, where we operate;
  • child sexual abuse material — we report it to the authorities;
  • phishing, credential harvesting, or impersonating another person, company or service;
  • malware, or command-and-control for it;
  • fraud, scams, or deceptive payment flows;
  • material that infringes someone else's intellectual property or privacy;
  • harassment, threats, or incitement to violence.

2. Outbound requests and email

On paid plans your handlers can make HTTP requests and send webhooks and email. Do not use them to:

  • send unsolicited bulk or commercial email, or email to addresses that did not ask for it;
  • scan, probe or attack systems you are not authorised to test;
  • take part in, amplify or relay denial-of-service traffic;
  • scrape services against their terms, or evade their rate limits.

Outbound requests are rate-limited per plan and cannot reach private or internal network addresses. Do not try to get around either.

3. The platform and other customers

  • Do not try to read, alter or disrupt another customer's apps or data, or to break out of the isolation between apps.
  • Do not mine cryptocurrency, or run work designed to consume shared resources rather than serve requests. Handlers run under time and memory limits per request.
  • Do not get around plan limits — for example by spreading one workload across several free accounts.
  • Do not resell the service or offer it as hosting to others without our written agreement.

Security research in good faith, within the scope of our DECISION: link /security once #327 publishes disclosure policy, is welcome and is not a breach of this policy.

4. What we do about it

We do not monitor app content in general. We act on reports and on what our systems flag. Depending on how serious it is, we may:

  • pause an app automatically: one that repeatedly runs past its per-request time limit answers "unavailable" for a short period, without notice. The pause lifts by itself, or as soon as you deploy a fix;
  • disable outbound requests for an account;
  • suspend an app: it stops serving, and nothing in it is deleted, so it can be restored;
  • end the account, as the Terms describe;
  • report illegal activity to the relevant authorities.

We will tell you what we did and why, by email, unless doing so would help the abuse continue or the law prevents it. If you think we got it wrong, reply to that email and a person will review it.

5. Reporting abuse

To report an app on rewind.js, write to DECISION: abuse@loop46.com or support@loop46.com (operated by Loop46, Inc.) with the URL and what you saw. Report security vulnerabilities to security@loop46.com instead.
