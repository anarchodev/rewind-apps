Status

Last updated 15 October 2026

Live status: status.rewindjs.com

Our status page runs on separate hosting that shares no servers, no datacenter and no code with rewind.js or this website; only its name is in our DNS. A status page that goes down with the thing it reports on is no use at the moment you need it, so it is the one part of rewind.js we deliberately do not run on rewind.js. If this page does not load, the status page still will.

What it checks

Every five minutes, from outside our network, it checks that these answer:

  • rewindjs.com — this website;
  • app.rewindjs.com — the dashboard;
  • auth.rewindjs.com — sign-in;
  • docs.rewindjs.com — the documentation;
  • an app on rewindjs.app — a test app of ours, served the same way as yours.

It publishes the response history it measures. We do not edit it, and we do not quote an uptime figure anywhere else.

When something breaks

  • Who declares an incident: the operator on call. Today that is one person, so outside working hours in the Pacific time zone an incident may go unacknowledged until morning. The automated checks mark an outage on the status page either way.
  • Where we post: on the status page — what is affected, what we know, and what we are doing.
  • How often: when we learn something new, and at least every 24 hours until it is resolved.
  • Afterwards: for an outage that affected customers, a short write-up of what happened and what we changed, on the status page.
  • If your data is affected by a security incident, we email you directly, as our data processing agreement requires; the status page is not the only notice.

What to expect during beta

There is no service-level agreement and no uptime commitment during beta (see our Terms). All three of our servers are in one datacenter, so an event at that site takes the whole service down until it is restored. Recovery would come from the last daily backup, so up to a day of changes could be lost, and we have not yet measured how long such a recovery takes. We would rather you knew that now.

To report a problem the status page has not caught, write to support@loop46.com (Loop46, Inc., the company that operates rewind.js).
