Support

Last updated 2 August 2026

rewind.js is early and small. There is no support queue behind a chat widget — there is an inbox, and a person reading it. The upside of that is you will not be triaged by a bot; the downside is the response times below are real, not aspirational.

Where to write

AddressForResponse
support@rewindjs.com Anything about using the platform — a handler behaving oddly, a deploy that will not take, a question the docs do not answer. 2 working days
security@rewindjs.com Suspected vulnerabilities. See below before you write. 2 working days to acknowledge
privacy@rewindjs.com Access, correction, deletion, export, or anything else in the privacy policy. 5 working days to acknowledge, 30 to complete

Reporting a security issue

Please report privately to security@rewindjs.com rather than opening a public issue, and give us a reasonable window to fix it before disclosing.

We run untrusted JavaScript from many tenants on shared infrastructure, so anything crossing a tenant boundary — reading another tenant's data, escaping the execution sandbox, reaching the control plane — is the class of report we care most about. We do not run a bug bounty and cannot pay for reports; we will credit you if you would like to be credited.

Bugs in the engine

rewind.js runs on an open-source engine. If you have found a bug in it and it is not a security issue, an issue on github.com/anarchodev/rove is the most useful place to put it — it goes straight to the people who would fix it, and other people can find it.

What we cannot help with

We can tell you what the platform did, and we can usually show you the recording of it. We cannot debug your application logic for you, and we will not read your tenant's data to investigate something unless you ask us to and tell us what to look at.

rewind.js is pre-launch. There is no paid support, no service level agreement, and no guaranteed availability. If you are weighing whether to put something important on it, that is the honest answer.
